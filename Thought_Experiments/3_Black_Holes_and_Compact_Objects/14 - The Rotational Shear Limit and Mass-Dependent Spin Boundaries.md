## Thought Experiment 14: The Rotational Shear Limit and Mass-Dependent Spin Boundaries

**The Scenario:**

Furthermore, it attributes sudden rotational shifts in neutron stars ("glitches") to hypothetical internal crust fractures. *"When a dense object spins at extreme velocities, it eventually hits a threshold where the surrounding spatial tension simply cannot sustain the rotation, triggering a violent snap-back through shear forces. By looking at this from a mass standpoint—recognizing that black holes are finite physical volumes rather than singularities—we can deduce that the more massive an object is, the lower its absolute limit of rotation. This mandates a specific geometric curve, dictating strict spin limits based entirely on an object's mass."*

**Substrate Architecture: Mass, Radius, and Transverse Shear:**

Consequently, a 10-solar-mass black hole must possess a significantly larger physical radius than a 2-solar-mass neutron star.
- **Tangential Boundary Drag:** The surrounding spatial nodes do not register Rotations Per Minute (RPM); they react exclusively to the tangential velocity of the object's physical boundary. As an ultra-dense mass spins, its boundary drags against the adjacent spatial medium, continuously injecting topological skew ($\text{ש}_S$) into the local dynamic connectivity.
- **The Mass-to-Spin Inversion:** Because it possesses a larger physical radius, the outer boundary of a more massive object covers vastly more physical distance per rotation. Consequently, a massive object generates severe topological drag at a much lower RPM than a smaller object. The absolute rotational speed limit is inversely proportional to the mass.
- **The Spatial Recoil (The Glitch):** When the tangential drag of the mass winds the local spatial connectivity to its absolute transverse shear ceiling ($\text{ש}_{S-MAX}$), the medium physically cannot absorb another degree of rotational tension. The adjacent nodes violently snap back to a lower-stress topological configuration. This spatial reset forces the mass to instantly stutter and synchronize with the newly relaxed geometry, shedding the excess rotational energy outward as a transverse shear ripple ($\Delta \text{ש}_S$) propagating at the fundamental information limit ($\text{ש}_I$).
