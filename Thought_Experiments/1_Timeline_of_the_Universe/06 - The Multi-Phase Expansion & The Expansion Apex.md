**The Scenario:**

Standard cosmology attempts to reconcile this fracture through ad-hoc early dark energy patches, while predicting infinite, runaway expansion driven by an unchanging cosmological constant ($\Lambda$). **The Principal Architect's Key Insight:**
> _"When the Big Bang happened, two things occurred: the rebound and the explosion. This caused space to massively expand initially, pushed by the explosion. As that power spread out, the expansion slowed significantly—almost as a mini snapback—eventually becoming what it is today. The overall expansion rate will increase to a point, and then it will start slowing down because there is less space to expand that hasn't already reached its maximum stretch. The overall curve bends up and then bends down. At the end, I don't think the universe reaches its maximum expansion and stops violently because there's nowhere else to go; it comes to a slow, grinding halt, stops, and then snaps back on itself."_

**The Four-Phase Elastomechanical Lifecycle:** 
The framework replaces a static cosmological constant with a dynamic four-phase mechanical progression governed by the zero-sum elasticity of the spatial network:
- **Phase 1: The Rebound & Explosive Surge (Kinetic Overshoot):** The primal bounce from the absolute compression floor ($\text{ש}_{MIN}$) releases a massive burst of kinetic pressure, amplified by the explosive decompression of trapped baryonic mass. This localized blast forces the spatial medium to rapidly stretch, massively overshooting its natural mechanical equilibrium.
- **Phase 2: The Substrate Snapback & Ringing (Volumetric Deceleration):** As the explosive kinetic pressure dilutes, the severe over-extension triggers an immediate elastic restoring reaction. The elastomechanical substrate pulls back toward its natural equilibrium ($\text{ש}_{REST}$). This abrupt deceleration forces the spatial network to ring, generating the longitudinal volumetric compression pulses ($\Delta \text{ש}_V$) that sort the initial cosmic web. Early-universe CMB measurements sample this decelerating, settling phase.
- **Phase 3: Localized Void Decompression (The Two-Point Fallacy):** Free from the chaotic violence of the early eon, the spatial substrate enters a secondary, steady decompression driven by late-stage structural accretion. As galaxies merge, supermassive black holes grow, and galactic clusters bind tighter, this ongoing gravitational consolidation forces localized nodes even closer to the physical Planck floor ($\text{ש}_{MIN}$). Because the substrate is governed by strict volumetric conservation, this late-stage macroscopic compression mathematically forces the remaining unburdened spatial nodes within macro-scale cosmic voids to stretch further toward their tensile ceiling ($\text{ש}_{MAX}$) to balance the ledger. Local distance ladder measurements sample this void-dominated regime, naturally producing a higher apparent $H_0$. Standard cosmology compares this higher local metric to the slower early-universe CMB metric and falsely assumes the entire universe is perpetually accelerating. The FSM reveals this is a topographical and phase-based discrepancy, not proof of a globally accelerating runaway metric.
- **Phase 4: The Saturated Apex & Graceful Throttling:** The universe cannot expand indefinitely. As an increasing percentage of void nodes reach their absolute volumetric limit ($\text{ש}_{MAX}$), the geometric capacity for further expansion steadily saturates. The internal elastic restoring tension of the substrate reasserts dominance, progressively throttling the expansion rate. The cosmos smoothly coasts to a complete, grinding halt at the Expansion Apex ($\dot{a} = 0$), where maximum stored elastic potential energy primes the substrate for the subsequent eon's inward collapse.
