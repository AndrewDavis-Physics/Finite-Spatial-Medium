**Substrate Architecture & Volumetric Mechanics:**
- **The Dual Kinetic Impulse (The Rebound and Matter Detonation):** Early hyper-expansion is not space being generated out of an abstract void; it is a compounded physical uncompression within a finite elastomechanical medium:
    - **Substrate Spring-Back:** Under universal contraction, inward-cascading collapse drives the medium toward its absolute geometric coordinate floor, the Physical Planck Floor ($\text{ש}_V \ge \text{ש}_{\text{MIN}}$). Because individual spatial nodes are fundamentally incompressible past this structural floor ($\Delta \text{ש}_V = 0$), the medium permanently forbids zero-volume mathematical singularities. The immense stored elastomechanical potential drives an aggressive rebound. As established in Postulate III, this rebound is intrinsically asynchronous—core over-densities lock and rebound outward while outer regions are still falling inward, generating directional kinetic shockwaves.
    - **Primordial Matter Detonation:** When dense matter is present at maximum compression, unspooled Primordial Matter violently detonates and begins re-nucleating simultaneously with the turnaround. While there is not enough baryonic matter in the cosmos to populate every individual node, maximum contraction drains all intervening volume out of the medium: empty, matterless void nodes are compressed down to the physical floor ($\text{ש}_V \to \text{ש}_{\text{MIN}}$) alongside matter-bearing nodes. Because macroscopic distances cease to exist at this boundary, matterless nodes sit directly adjacent to the detonating mass seeds. The omnidirectional kinetic blast couples directly into these tightly packed neighboring nodes, mechanically driving the entire contiguous medium outward in the violent, non-linear displacement epoch conventionally labeled "inflation."
- **Depletion of the Kinetic Push:** The matter detonation is a finite kinetic release. As matter rapidly dilutes across the opening volume of the substrate, the explosive blast pressure drops precipitously. The extra push is self-limiting: once matter disperses across space, the outward driver behind the hyper-acceleration runs out of steam.
- **Spatial Inertia and Structural Resistance (Space Fights Back):** The spatial medium is a physical, energy-conserving substrate that possesses its own intrinsic inertia and discrete state-change threshold ($\text{ש}_I$). It cannot undergo extreme, unnatural displacement velocities without generating severe internal structural strain:
    - While the exploding matter actively drives the expansion, the substrate is forced outward far faster than its natural elastic relaxation rate.
    - The moment the matter blast disperses and loses its outward push, the intrinsic elastomechanical resistance and structural inertia of the spatial medium take over.
    - The medium physically resists this unnatural displacement velocity, acting as an elastomechanical brake that forces an immediate, steep collapse in the expansion rate of change ($\Delta H / \Delta t$). This built-in structural resistance throttles the expansion back down to the medium's natural, sustainable coasting curve without requiring arbitrary scalar fields.
