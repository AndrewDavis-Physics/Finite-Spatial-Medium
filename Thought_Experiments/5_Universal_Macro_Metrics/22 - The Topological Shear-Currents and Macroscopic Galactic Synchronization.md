**Substrate Architecture & The Macroscopic Pinwheel Mechanics:**
Under the framework's discrete mechanics, galaxies do not spin in an isolated vacuum. Their angular momentum is heavily dictated by the local topological shear ($\text{ש}_S$) and the specific elastomechanical environment of the filament they reside in:
- **The Unbroken Acoustic Shear Line (The Quasar Lock):** Under the FSM's cosmic initialization mechanics, the massive filaments of the Cosmic Web are the physical acoustic standing waves left over from the primordial rebound. A filament is a contiguous, unbroken structural trough in the spatial medium. Because the entire spatial substrate carries a universal macroscopic rotation, these massive linear troughs possess a unified topological shear bias ($\text{ש}_S$) that runs completely down their length. Quasars separated by billions of light-years are aligned because they are anchored to the exact same acoustic shear line; they are governed by the unified structural "grain" of the continuous filament.
- **The Pipeline Roll (Low-Mass Parallel Alignment):** In a low-mass, sparse filament, the dominant mechanical force is the longitudinal flow of matter. Driven by the framework's topographic velocity currents, these regions act as elastomechanical pipelines, where upstream void decompression pushes matter smoothly downstream. As baryonic gas flows longitudinally down this topological pipeline, the path of least resistance naturally rolls the condensing gas into a vortex parallel to the current—exactly like water spiraling as it flows through a pipe. Therefore, galaxies in low-mass filaments natively spin parallel to the axis.
- **The Cavitation Crush (High-Mass Perpendicular Alignment):** In a high-mass, dense filament, the dynamics radically change. Within the FSM, massive supercluster filaments are the catastrophic collision boundaries of expanding supervoids (secondary cavitation bubbles). Here, matter is not flowing smoothly down a pipe; it is being aggressively bulldozed and crushed _inward_ toward the filament axis from the opposing lateral flanks. When matter violently converges from opposing lateral sides, the extreme localized spatial compression ($\text{ש}_V \to \text{ש}_{MIN}$) forces the resulting angular momentum to shear at exactly $90^\circ$ to the crush plane. Therefore, galaxies born in these extreme collision zones natively spin perpendicular to the filament.
