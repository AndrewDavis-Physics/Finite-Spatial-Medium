## Thought Experiment 21: The Elastomechanical Slipstream and the Planar Disk of Satellites

**The Scenario:** 
According to standard continuous cosmology ($\Lambda\text{CDM}$), large galaxies form inside massive, spherical halos of invisible Cold Dark Matter. Because this hypothetical matter is assumed to have accreted chaotically over billions of years, the dwarf satellite galaxies orbiting the Milky Way, Andromeda, and Centaurus A should be swarming randomly in all directions. Instead, high-precision astronomical telemetry reveals a severe structural crisis: these satellite galaxies are organized into razor-thin, highly structured planar disks, and they co-rotate in unison. Standard vacuum physics has no mechanism to force dozens of independent dwarf galaxies into a rigid, planar formation. The Finite Spatial Medium resolves this anomaly by modeling the Local Group not as an isolated cluster in a vacuum, but as a cohesive, multi-body tension web transiting along a macroscopic topological velocity current.

The entire local group is moving together down this pipeline, and the dwarf galaxies are simply aligning with the tension created by that unified movement."_

The entire Local Group is cutting a massive, unified wake through the spatial fluid.

This shared multi-body compression creates a highly specific geometric stress profile in the permeating spatial network.

Instead, a dwarf galaxy maintaining a chaotic, high-inclination orbit through the Local Group's massive transit path is constantly subjected to asymmetric, overlapping topological shear ($\text{ש}_S$). Over billions of years, this continuous macroscopic torque mechanically steers the satellite’s orbital inclination. The dwarf galaxies are natively funneled into the shared plane of least topological resistance—the geometric "slipstream" of the massive host. The razor-thin disk of satellites is not a random dark matter accretion; it is the physical, geometric harmonic of multiple mass anchors navigating the exact same substrate current, aligning structurally without bleeding orbital kinetic energy.
