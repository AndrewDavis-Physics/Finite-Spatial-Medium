# The Finite Spatial Medium (FSM) — Thought Experiments Archive

A discrete, volume-conserving elastomechanical physical substrate replacing the continuous spacetime manifold.

### About the Author:
I am a career Microsoft systems administrator and infrastructure architect. My background is not academic: I joined the United States Air Force directly out of high school, never attended college, and never took a formal physics class. My formal mathematics education ended when I graduated high school in 2000.

As a result, I don't communicate through the shorthand of academic physics or advanced calculus. If we talk about these concepts, I'll often ask to discuss things in terms of tangible physical mechanics rather than textbook terminology.

What I bring to this work is decades of practical experience diagnosing complex distributed systems, physical networks, capacity ceilings, and latency bottlenecks. I approach physics not as an abstract geometric exercise, but as a physical architecture with real mechanical components, strict volume conservation, and hardware-style update limits.

I understand the physical logic of this framework inside and out because I built it from the ground up to operate as a coherent machine. My goal here is open collaboration: I welcome working with researchers, mathematicians, and numerical analysts who can help translate these physical mechanics into the formal mathematical models and structure-preserving code needed to test them rigorously.
